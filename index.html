About the Day

One day. The whole school.

Rosenstiel Research Day is the annual school-wide gathering of the Rosenstiel School of Marine, Atmospheric, and Earth Science. The day brings together students, postdocs, staff, and faculty for a full program of oral talks, lightning talks, and posters spanning the breadth of the school's science — from seismology and geodynamics to physical and biological oceanography, atmospheric science, climate, marine ecosystems, and the tools that change how we do science.

Scope of Science

From the deep ocean to the upper atmosphere.

Rosenstiel research spans the full Earth system. Research Day brings these communities under one roof for a single day of cross-disciplinary exchange.

Program at a Glance

What the day includes.

Oral Sessions

Four sessions of full-length research talks (10 min + 2 min Q&A) in the Auditorium.

Lightning Talks

Three sessions of rapid-fire 3-minute talks highlighting key findings — hard stop.

Poster Sessions

Morning and afternoon poster sessions in SALT with refreshments and dedicated discussion time.

Awards & Social Hour

Research & Presentation Excellence Awards followed by a closing social hour in SALT.

Research & Presentation Excellence

Awards that recognize the best of Rosenstiel.

A Tradition of Excellence

Five awards. Chosen by attendee vote.

Research Day closes with a recognition ceremony honoring presentations across categories — from scientific storytelling to lightning-talk impact.

People's Choice

Voted by attendees for the presentation that most captured the audience's attention.

Outstanding Student Presentation

For a student-led presentation demonstrating scientific excellence and effective communication.

Most Memorable Lightning Talk

For a lightning talk that stood out for clarity, creativity, and lasting impact.

Best Science Storytelling

For communicating scientific ideas with exceptional clarity and narrative flow.

Best Visual Design

For outstanding visual clarity and design that enhanced scientific understanding.

Everyone Is Invited
