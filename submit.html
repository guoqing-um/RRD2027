Call for Abstracts

Submit an Abstract

Students, postdocs, staff, and faculty across the Rosenstiel School are invited to present as an oral talk, lightning talk, or poster.

Submission is your registration. When you submit an abstract you are automatically registered for Research Day — there is no separate registration step. Not presenting? Use the audience-only sign-up instead.
Submission deadline: Friday, December 18, 2026 at 5:00 pm ET.

Presentation Formats

Oral Talk

10 minutes presentation + 2 minutes Q&A. Best for in-depth results in a focused area.

Lightning Talk

3 minutes, hard stop. Best for sharing a key finding, idea, or tool in a punchy format.

Poster

Morning or afternoon poster session in SALT. Best for visual results and one-on-one discussion.

Guidelines

  • Length: 250 words maximum, excluding title and author list.
  • Format: Plain text. No figures or equations in the abstract body.
  • Authors: List all authors with affiliations. Underline or otherwise mark the presenting author.
  • Format preference: Indicate your preferred presentation type (oral / lightning / poster). Assignments may be adjusted based on capacity and fit.
  • Eligibility: Open to all members of the Rosenstiel School — students, postdocs, staff, and faculty — and collaborating groups.

What the Form Will Ask

Since submission also registers you for the day, the form collects both your abstract and the logistics we'd otherwise ask at registration:

  • You: name, email, role (student / postdoc / staff / faculty), department, PI / advisor.
  • Your abstract: title, author list (mark the presenting author), abstract body, subject area keywords.
  • Format: preferred format (oral / lightning / poster) and whether you'd accept an alternative if your first choice is full.
  • Logistics: equipment / AV needs, dietary restrictions, accessibility needs, lunch & social-hour headcount.
  • Consent: photo / video consent for Rosenstiel communications.

Submit Online

Complete the form below. You will receive an email confirmation within one business day confirming both your abstract and your registration. Submission opens Tuesday, November 3, 2026.

Trouble with the form? You may also submit by emailing your abstract as a plain-text or PDF attachment to glin@miami.edu.

Review & Notification

Submissions will be grouped by theme and assigned to oral, lightning, or poster format. All authors will be notified of session placement in early-to-mid January 2027.

Presenters will be asked to finalize titles and any poster needs by Friday, January 8, 2027.
