Call for Abstracts

Submit an Abstract

Students, postdocs, and staff across the Rosenstiel School are invited to present an oral talk, or a poster (with an optional flash talk). Faculty give a 1-minute intro (Faculty page) and may also submit an abstract to present a poster.

Submission is your registration. When you submit an abstract you are automatically registered for Research Day, there is no separate registration step. Faculty: your 1-minute intro needs no abstract (Faculty page), but you may submit one here to present a poster, faculty submissions are placed in the poster track. Not presenting? Use the audience-only sign-up instead.
Submission deadline: Friday, December 18, 2026 at 5:00 pm ET.

Presentation Formats

Oral Talk

10 minutes, selective. Best for in-depth results, for students, postdocs, and staff.

Poster (+ optional flash)

A poster in a dedicated viewing block, with an optional flash teaser (encouraged). Open to everyone, including faculty.

Guidelines

  • Length: 250 words maximum, excluding title and author list.
  • Format: Plain text. No figures or equations in the abstract body.
  • Authors: List all authors with affiliations. Underline or otherwise mark the presenting author.
  • Format preference: Indicate your preferred type (oral or poster). Poster presenters may add an optional flash talk (encouraged); assignments may be adjusted based on capacity and fit.
  • Eligibility: Open across the Rosenstiel School and collaborating groups. Oral slots are for students, postdocs, and staff; faculty may submit too and are assigned to the poster track. Faculty also give a 1-minute intro, see the Faculty page.

What the Form Will Ask

Since submission also registers you for the day, the form collects both your abstract and the logistics we'd otherwise ask at registration:

  • You: name, email, role (student / postdoc / staff / faculty), department, PI / advisor.
  • Your abstract: title, author list (mark the presenting author), abstract body, subject area keywords.
  • Format: preferred format (oral, or poster with or without a flash talk) and whether you'd accept an alternative if your first choice is full.
  • Logistics: equipment / AV needs, dietary restrictions, accessibility needs, lunch & social-hour headcount.
  • Consent: photo / video consent for Rosenstiel communications.

Submit Online

Submission opens Thursday, October 1, 2026. You'll receive an email confirmation within one business day confirming both your abstract and your registration.

Open the Abstract Submission Form

Trouble with the form? You may also submit by emailing your abstract as a plain-text or PDF attachment to glin@miami.edu.

Review & Notification

Submissions will be grouped by theme and assigned to oral or flash-plus-poster format. All authors will learn their placement when the program is announced on Monday, January 25, 2027.

Presenters will be asked to finalize titles and any poster needs by Friday, January 8, 2027.

Research & Presentation Excellence

Awards.

A Tradition of Excellence

Four awards, by attendee vote.

Winners are chosen by attendee vote through the post-event survey and announced about two weeks after the event – people's choice, best oral presentation, best poster, and the cross-currents award for bridging disciplines.

People's Choice

Voted by attendees for the presentation that most captured the audience's attention.

Best Oral Presentation

For the oral talk that best combined scientific excellence with clear communication.

Best Poster

For the poster that pairs scientific depth with standout clarity and discussion.

Cross-Currents Award

For the work that best bridges two departments or disciplines across the school.
