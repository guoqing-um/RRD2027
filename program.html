Program

A tentative schedule for the day. The whole school is present throughout, Meet the Faculty, oral, and flash sessions all mix presenters across departments. Final times and presenters will be confirmed about a week before the event.

Tentative schedule. Here's the shape of the day. The Centers Showcase, Meet the Faculty, oral talks, and flash sessions take place in the Auditorium; lunch, posters, and the social hour are in SALT; check-in is in the MSC Lobby. The whole school is together throughout the day, with the oral, flash, and Meet the Faculty sessions drawing presenters from every department. Times are approximate and may shift slightly, the final program will be posted about a week before the event.
