For Faculty

Meet the Faculty

Every faculty member opens a Meet the Faculty round with a 1-minute introduction — a quick "who I am" and a snapshot of your research. No abstract is needed for the intro. Want to present research too? You may submit an abstract — faculty submissions are placed in the poster track.

No abstract required for the intro. Just sign up below and bring one slide — we'll place you in a Meet the Faculty round. If you'd also like to present a poster, submit an abstract on the Submit Abstract page; faculty submissions are assigned to the poster track (oral slots are reserved for students, postdocs, and staff).
Sign-up closes: Friday, December 18, 2026 at 5:00 pm ET.

How the Intro Works

  • One minute, hard stop. A single slide auto-advances after 60 seconds, so the round stays brisk.
  • Self-definition opening. Open with one line on who you are, then a snapshot of your research and what you're looking for (collaborators, students, data).
  • One slide. Send a single slide (title + one visual). A template will be provided after you sign up.
  • Two rounds. Meet the Faculty runs once in the morning and once in the afternoon; you'll be placed in one round. The whole school is present throughout.

Faculty Sign-Up Form

A short form — no abstract. It asks for your name, department, a one-line self-definition for the program, and confirmation you'll send a slide. It also collects lunch & social-hour headcount and dietary needs.

Open the Faculty Sign-Up Form

Prefer email? Send your name, department, a one-line self-definition, and whether you'll attend lunch and the social hour to glin@miami.edu.

Other Ways Faculty Take Part

  • Present a poster. Beyond the intro, you're welcome to submit an abstract — faculty submissions are placed in the poster track.
  • Mentor & chair. Encourage your students and postdocs to submit, and help chair a session.
  • Vote on the awards. Faculty vote alongside everyone in the post-event survey; winners are announced about two weeks after the event.
  • Show up for the posters. The two dedicated poster viewing blocks are where student presenters most value faculty attention.

Have Students or Postdocs Presenting?

Students, postdocs, and staff present a talk or a poster — and they register by submitting an abstract on the Submit Abstract page. Point your group there.
